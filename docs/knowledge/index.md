# Knowledge

This is the long-lived output of the catalog: market research that feeds the
`C` (competition) scores, and the reasons specific ideas were investigated and
rejected. Code and scores change; the reasoning here is meant to last.

- [Learnings](learnings/) — market research and methodology notes that inform
  scoring, especially the `C` axis.
- [Failed approaches](failed-approaches/) — ideas that were flushed out and
  then rejected, with the specific reason. Read these before re-investigating
  the same idea.

## Relationship to the catalog

The catalog (`scripts/build.py`) records **what** the scores are. The knowledge
tree records **why** — the market structure, incumbent funding, and feasibility
reasoning that produced those scores. When a score changes, the reasoning that
drove the change should land here (or in an
[ADR](../architecture/decisions/) if it is a model-level change).
