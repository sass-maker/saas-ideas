# AI Ideas

B3 – Recruiter Candidate Intelligence
You already think about resumes/jobs. Small recruiters and in-house HR have clear pain and budgets. Easy to price, easy to demo ROI.

A1 – Synthetic User QA Tester
QA is a real line item. Self-serve $50–$200/mo per team is believable. Competition exists, but the value story is simple.

B2 – AI Postmortem & RCA Generator
Narrow, painful, and mandated in any semi-serious org. Turns hated work into editing. Good wedge into SRE/infra budgets.

B1 – AI Log & Incident Copilot
Outage/incident cost is huge, so value is clear. But this space is noisy, and expectations are high.

A4 – Negotiation/Sales Roleplay Partner
Corporate L&D spends a lot. But you need sales motion and proof. Still, even a few teams at $200–$500/mo each matters.

B5 – Customer Interview Digest
PMs/founders already pay for Gong/Chorus/tl;dv. If you’re “cheaper and simpler”, you can carve out a niche.

B6 – AI Knowledge Ops
Big TAM, but swamped with competitors and requires selling into mid-sized companies/teams. Higher friction.

C2 – Screen/Browser Copilot
Could get B2C subs from devs/researchers. Real but not obviously huge; requires UX polish and growth loops.

B4 – AI Step-Through Debugger
Strong devtool but devtools are hard to monetize early without distribution. More “strategic” than “quick cash”.

A3 – Deep Dive Investment Analyst
Very high ARPU if it works, but you don’t have the VC/M&A network yet. Long sales cycles, trust barrier.

A2 – Legacy Resurrection
Enterprises could pay 5–6 figures, but they will not trust a random new tool with their core systems. Multi-year credibility play.

C1 – LLM Experiment & Routing Hub
Devs love it; devs also love not paying. Many will self-host or hack their own. Monetizable, but you’ll fight “I can just script this”.

B7 – LLM Benchmarks & Cost Optimizer
Nice-to-have. Budgets tiny unless you evolve into a full “LLM ops” platform, which is crowded already.

C3 – Builder Brain
Emotionally compelling, but users don’t pay reliably. This is more “cool toy” than business unless turned into a team product.

C4 – Small Incident/Log Explainer
As a standalone, it’s just a feature of B1, not a business.
