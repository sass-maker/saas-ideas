# SaaS Ideas

A scored, sortable catalog of tech-heavy SaaS product ideas aimed at solo
builders targeting $5K MRR.

## How ideas are compared

- Fun combines the subjective build interest and technical challenge scores.
- Money combines raw earning potential, solo feasibility, and competition.
- Best bets exclude developer-only audiences and require a strong fun or money
  score.
- The published list is hard-filtered to technical challenge scores of 7 or
  higher.

Scores are comparative decision-support signals, not forecasts or guarantees.
