# magicform

- can have prebuilt templates, can let users publish them as well.
- CustomForm with AI: You can get json structures from gpt responses, allowing you to create forms with very ease (google forms or custom). Summarize the response to make better sense easily.
- Form products do great to generate domain rank. AI based filtering, you can just write what you are looking for, AI will generate the form which you can tweak and confirm.
- A form for everything - recruitment, surveys, data collections, rsvps, customer support tickets etc.
- All form entries should also technically allow for 2 way communication. Future form updates are automatically broadcasted and asked to fill by others.
- More options to reference: E-commerce Checkout, Account Creation, Contact Forms, Subscription Signups, Booking Systems, Contests or Giveaways, Customer Support Tickets, Product Pre-orders, Donation Forms, Quizzes or Assessments.
- As overkill features: realtime collaboration & forms maintain history. Custom caching for different forms response sizes. Broadcast messages based on form response filters. Also should be pluggable everywhere. And provide relevant api and webhook events.
- Can add AI step in pipeline
- Form level analytics (opened time, submitted time, source etc). Active time spent of form filling (can be gamed). These analytics can be based on versioning.
- Can require email based form access.
- Can eventually made such that, it revolutionizes user input (can consider calls as well). Also can use storage sass for better data understanding.
- A good form experience can help the filler be exposed to your product and remember it when they have to fill the form again.
- Can save user's response to similar questions and auto fill

- Can have an client sdk which just takes formId (given to user after successful publish) and then they can just add the form anywhere with various styling. Can let users attach design screenshots and then attempt to design the form based on it. (so form should accept bunch of styling classes)\
- Can give feedback to user's created form
